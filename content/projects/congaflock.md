## Overview

CONGAFLOCK explores how an externally illuminated circuit can reflect information about a connected signal. The original page reports a working prototype at DEF CON 22.

## Working with the original project

The upstream retroreflector collection includes its PCB design. The original source remains the reference for hardware details and experimental setup.
