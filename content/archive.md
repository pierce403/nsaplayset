## Recovery status

This is a partial reconstruction, assembled on September 29, 2026 from available project sources, indexed mirror pages, and historical links. The original domain and Wayback archive could not be fetched reliably during this pass. This site does not claim to be a complete mirror.

The [GBPPR mirror](https://gbppr.net/nsaplayset/) preserves parts of the original site. Project pages link to their individual sources. New summaries are clearly separated from original upstream documentation.

## Missing downloads

The files at these historical URLs have not yet been recovered into this site:

- `/ossmann_hitb2014.pdf` — original HITB 2014 presentation.
- `/nsa_ant_catalog.pdf` — ANT catalog PDF.
- `/twilightvegetable/NSA-PLAYSET-GSM.pdf` — GSM presentation located in the mirror; local recovery pending.

Requests for these paths temporarily redirect to this explanation. They do not return replacement files. Once the exact documents are recovered, the redirects should be removed and the files served from their original paths.
